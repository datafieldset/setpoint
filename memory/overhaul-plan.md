# Signal overhaul plan (Nov 11, Na: "ok do it and push")

Goal: at least one signal we can stand behind, because the signals are what Setpoint sells. "Reliable" means a positive average after costs on enough trades, in more than one market period, then confirmed live. It does not mean it wins every time.

Rules are written here BEFORE results are looked at. Do not move the bar after seeing a number.

## Pass bar (same for every signal, live or hidden)
- Bitcoin only (standing rule).
- Costs: 0.15% round trip fee plus 0.05% slippage, so 0.20% per trade. Fees stay in our grading only, not in what customers see (Na, Oct 11).
- Positive after costs in every era that has at least 8 trades: 2020-21, 2022, 2023-24, 2025 (to the lock date).
- Enough trades: 100+ on charts under 1 hour, 50+ on 1 hour and slower.
- Still positive with its best 5% of trades removed.
- Beats the luck test: clearly better than random entries with the same stop and target, taken in the same trend conditions.
- Then the locked exam: the most recent 12 months of data (Nov 7 2025 to Nov 6 2026) are graded once, at the end.
- Honest limit: Swing Rider and Flush Rider were designed with those 12 months in view, so the exam is only clean for new ideas. For those two the real exam is the live record.

## Steps
1. Scoreboard: replay every signal under the rules above, with the luck test. Retire what fails (list it for Na first, retiring is a code change and needs his yes).
2. Stress-test Swing Rider and Flush Rider: worse fills, shifted start dates, losing streaks, drawdown, time in trade, 1-minute data for which-was-hit-first, Coinbase prices instead of Binance.
3. A few improvements from a short written list (below), each counted, each judged on data it was not tuned on (tune on one stretch, test on the next, roll forward).
4. Look for a third candidate, a few ideas only: slow charts, bigger targets, trend filters. Biggest gap: every live signal is a buy and most go quiet under the 200-day, so there is nothing for a bear market.
5. Live proof: sellable at 20+ live trades with a positive average; compare live to replay monthly.

## Second and third signal hunt (Nov 11 night, Na: "12 trades a year is not enough to charge people... start building another alert that is more frequent"; also "look at what we discussed", meaning data beyond price, and the traditional indicators traders still use)
Target: a signal that passes the same bar AND fires at least about 40 times a year. Written here BEFORE running. Tries 20 to 27 (8 new), so 27 in total.
Data found reachable from the cloud: Binance futures funding rate (every 8 hours, Jan 2020 on) and Binance futures "metrics" (open interest, top-trader and all-account long/short ratios, taker buy/sell ratio, every 5 minutes, Sep 2020 on) from data.binance.vision. Bybit and BitMEX are blocked (403); OKX only keeps about 3 months. Eras for these ideas: Sep 2020-21, 2022, 2023-24, 2025 to the lock. All on Bitcoin, cost 0.20%, entry at the next bar's open, one trade at a time, only data finished before the entry.
- D1 Squeeze buy (1h): the latest funding print is negative (shorts are paying) and a 1h bar closes above the high of the prior 24 bars. Stop 2 ATR, target 3R.
- D2 Washout buy (1h): open interest fell 5% or more over the last 4 finished hours while price fell 2% or more over the same 4 hours. Stop 2 ATR, target 3R, 24 hour wait between entries.
- D3 Crowded-long sell (1h): funding at or above the 90th percentile of the last 90 days, open interest up 5% or more in 24 hours, and a 1h close under the low of the prior 24 bars. Stop 2 ATR, target 3R. (The bear-market gap.)
- D4 Fresh-money breakout buy (1h): close above the prior 48-bar high, open interest up 3% or more in 24 hours, funding below its 90-day median (not crowded yet). Stop 2 ATR, target 3R.
- D5 Crowd-is-short buy (4h): all-account long/short ratio in the lowest 10% of the last 30 days at a 4h close that is an up bar. Stop 2 ATR, target 3R.
- D6 Crowd-is-long sell (4h): the mirror (ratio in the highest 10%, a down bar, price under the 200-day). Stop 2 ATR, target 3R.
- T1 Pullback in an uptrend (1h, traditional): price above the 200-day and the 50-day is rising (above its value 10 days ago); 1h RSI(14) closed under 35 within the last 6 bars and a 1h bar now closes back above its 20-bar EMA. Stop 2 ATR, target 3R.
- T2 Yesterday's-high break (1h, traditional): price above the 200-day, yesterday closed above its 20-day average; the first 1h close of the day above yesterday's high. Stop 2 ATR, target 3R, one a day.
Not a new try: the looser Swing Rider (no "not stretched" check, about 25 a year, +0.35R) is already tested and is the fallback for frequency.
A pass needs the full bar, the luck test and the locked exam. With 27 tries, one lucky pass is expected by chance, so a pass also needs its neighbor settings to agree.
- Try 28, written Nov 11 after the first run and BEFORE running it: D4 with the project's standing buy rule added (price above the 200-day). Why: D4 was positive overall (+0.31R, 102 trades) and failed only on 2022, the bear year, where every buy idea in this project loses. This is a change made after seeing a result, so it only counts if the locked year and the neighbor settings agree. Also run as checks, not tries: the plain 48-bar breakout with the same stop and target (does the open-interest and funding part add anything?), and D4's neighbors (24 and 72 bars, open interest 2% and 5%, target 2R and 4R).

## Crash-short study (Nov 21, Na: BTC fell 17.1% Nov 11 to Nov 15 with no short signal, then "1 and 2 sounds good"). Study only, nothing built. Written BEFORE running.
Question: does any short on BTC clear the standing bar, when it only trades in a crash regime? Same bar as above (0.20% cost per trade, every era with 8+ trades positive, 50+ trades on 1h, still positive minus best 5%, luck test, locked exam), plus neighbors that agree. Tries 33 to 35 (35 in total).
Crash regime, measured on finished bars only (no look-ahead): price under its 200-day average AND under its 50-day average AND at least X% below its highest high of the last 7 days. X = 10% (main), 8% and 12% as neighbors. 200-day and 50-day are the last COMPLETED daily bars.
- Try 33, breakdown short (1h): in the regime, a 1h bar closes under the lowest low of the prior 24 bars. Enter next bar open. Stop 2 ATR, target 3R (neighbors 2R, 4R). One trade at a time, 12 bar pause after a trade ends.
- Try 34, failed-bounce short (1h): in the regime, a 1h bar closes under its 20-bar EMA after at least one close above it within the prior 6 bars. Enter next bar open. Stop 2 ATR, target 3R (neighbors 2R, 4R). Same gate.
- Try 35, same as 33 on the 4h chart (stop 2 ATR, target 3R, neighbors 2R, 4R), fewer but cleaner.
Luck test: the same entries (same stop, target, gate) taken at random bars inside the same regime, 200 draws with the same trade count; the real result must beat 95% of the draws. Also run the entries with NO regime as a check, not a try: if the regime does not help, say so.
Data: BTC 5m Jan 2020 to Nov 6 2026 (existing) plus Nov 6 to now (Binance mirror). Eras 2020-21, 2022, 2023-24, 2025 to Nov 6 2026. The locked exam stays Nov 7 2025 to Nov 6 2026. The crash itself (Nov 11 to Nov 15 2026) is AFTER the lock, so it is the one clean, untouched test: report what each try would have done that week, graded once, and never tune on it.
Known limit: 10% weekly drops are rare (few distinct episodes), so a pass on trade counts can still rest on a handful of crashes. Report how many separate episodes the trades come from.
Result (Nov 21, scripts/replay-crash-short.mjs, BTC 5m Jan 2020 to Nov 21 2026, cost 0.20%/trade). NO PASS. 27 variants run (3 tries x 3 drop sizes x 3 targets) plus a no-regime check.

## Bounce winner-vs-loser study (Nov 22, Na: "if we know we lose 63% the data should tell us what to change"). Written BEFORE running. Tries 47 to 56.
Data: every Bounce fill of the base rule (lib/flush.js plan, 3R target, 0.20% cost) on flushes of 1.5% or more (270 trades, includes the 124 at 2.5%) so there is enough to learn from. Ten things known at the close of the flush bar (before any buy): flush size, where the bar closed inside its own range, volume vs the prior 24h average, price change over the prior 24h and 72h, distance under the daily 50-day and 200-day averages, volatility (ATR as % of price), how far it broke under the 48h low, and whether it fell during US hours (13 to 21 UTC).
Test for each: split at the median, compare average R above vs below. A filter is kept only if (1) the same side is better in BOTH time halves, (2) it is better by 0.30R or more over the full set, and (3) it survives the family-wise luck test: shuffle the trade results 1,000 times and see how often the BEST of the ten features looks this good by chance (needs under 5%). Only then is the filtered rule graded: 50+ trades on the 1.5% set, every era with 8+ trades positive, minus best 5% positive, locked exam Nov 7 2025 to Nov 6 2026, and the crash week after Nov 6 graded once. If none pass, Bounce stays as it is.
Result (Nov 22, scripts/replay-bounce-filter.mjs and scripts/replay-bounce-oos.mjs): Of ten things known at the flush, the one that separates winners from losers is VOLATILITY (hourly ATR as % of price). Flush >= 1.5%, 270 trades 2020 to Nov 2026: ATR under 1.02% = -0.13R, over = +0.44R; same side in both halves (+0.92 / +0.55). Thresholds: no filter +0.15R (270); ATR >= 0.8% +0.23 (212); >= 1.0% +0.38 (142, -best5% +0.24, eras +0.27/+0.40/+0.71/+0.49, but locked exam n=12 -0.17R); >= 1.2% +0.41 (89); >= 1.5% +0.66 (50). Family-wise luck test FAILED the pre-set 5% gate: the best of ten looks this good by chance in 12.5% of shuffles. Related features agree (deeper under the 50-day -0.46R diff, deeper prior 72h drop -0.48R diff, bar closing near its low -0.29R diff); size, volume, break depth, US hours do not hold. OUT OF SAMPLE on BTC 1h Aug 2017 to Dec 2019 (never used to pick the filter; 140 trades at 1.5% flush, base rule -0.09R unfiltered): ATR >= 1.0% -0.05R (does not confirm), >= 1.2% +0.14R (93 trades, -best5% +0.02), >= 1.5% +0.28R (67 trades, -best5% +0.16, halves +0.01/+0.55). At 2.5% flush: unfiltered +0.07R; ATR >= 1.2% +0.25R (77), >= 1.5% +0.27R (62). Direction holds in both samples (more volatile = better bounce); size of the edge is uncertain. Mechanism (plausible, not proven): in a volatile market the 1 ATR stop is wide in percent, so the 0.20% cost and noise take a smaller share of each trade. Candidate "Bounce volatility gate": flush >= 1.5% (or the live 2.5%) AND hourly ATR >= 1.2% of price. Not a pass under the strict gates (luck 12.5%, exam n=5 -0.34R), so proposed as a logged test only; live Bounce unchanged. Crash week under the gate (1.5% flush, ATR >= 1.0%): only the Nov 21 05:00 UTC buy (+2.87R), the Nov 10 to Nov 14 losses are skipped; graded once, not tuned on.
Walk-forward on ALL BTC 1h Aug 2017 to Nov 2026 (scripts/replay-bounce-walkforward.mjs, replay-bounce-union.mjs; Nov 22, Na: "we have enough history, no logging"). Flush >= 1.5% base rule, cost 0.20%: 410 trades unfiltered +0.07R. Gate on hourly ATR as % of price: >= 1.0% 251 trades +0.19R (random-picking chance 5.7%); >= 1.2% 182 trades 35% win +0.27R, -best5% +0.13 (chance 2.3%, 9 of 10 years positive); >= 1.5% 117 trades 38% win +0.45R, -best5% +0.33 (chance 0.5%, 8 of 9 years positive, 13 a year). Walk-forward (each year chooses its gate from earlier years only; it always chose 1.5%): 2019 to now 62 trades +0.64R vs 315 unfiltered +0.13R, luck 0.8%. This PASSES the luck gate (under 5%) on the full history. Weak spot: the gate fires rarely in calm years (2024 to 2026: 4 trades at 1.5%, 18 at 1.2%). Live Bounce today (flush 2.5%, any volatility) on the same set: 182 trades +0.26R, -best5% +0.12, 7 of 10 years positive, about 20 a year. Gating a 2.5% flush helps less (13% to 18% chance, base already good). Union (2.5% any OR 1.5% + swing 1.5%+): 214 trades +0.26R, no better than today.
